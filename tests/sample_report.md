# Unveilr AI: Company Intelligence Report

## Executive summary

Unveilr AI is an early-stage Indian startup building **AI search agents** that help brands appear in answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews [S1]. It raised a pre-seed round from AJVC at a ₹16.7 crore valuation on 1 October 2026 [S1, S3]. The company sells a managed service rather than only a dashboard, which sets it apart from better-funded competitors [S3].

> Key takeaway: the round is small and the valuation modest, but the category (AI visibility / AEO) is attracting large rounds globally.

## Product and technology

- Monitors how often a brand is mentioned in AI-generated answers [S1]
- Combines traditional SEO with answer engine optimisation (AEO) [S1]
