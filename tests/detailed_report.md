# Unveilr AI Company Intelligence Report

## 1. Executive summary

Unveilr AI is an early-stage Indian startup building AI search agents that help brands appear in answers generated by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews [S1]. On 1 October 2026 it announced a pre-seed round led by AJVC at a ₹16.7 crore valuation; the amount raised was not disclosed [S2, S3]. The company sells a managed service in which its own team runs answer engine optimisation (AEO) programmes for clients, rather than selling a self-serve dashboard [S3].

The category is attracting substantial capital globally. Profound, the best-funded direct competitor, has raised a $96M Series C at a $1B valuation [S3]. Unveilr enters at a much smaller scale, and public evidence about its customers, revenue and technology is thin.

## 3. Products and services

Unveilr describes its offer as AI search visibility: measuring and improving how often a brand is mentioned, recommended or cited in AI-generated answers [S1].

- **Visibility monitoring**: tracks brand mentions across AI assistants [S1]
- **AEO and SEO programmes**: combines traditional search optimisation with answer engine optimisation [S1, S3]
  - Content restructuring for retrieval by AI systems
  - Share-of-voice reporting against named competitors
- **Managed delivery**: Unveilr's team executes the work for clients [S3]

### 3.1 Business model and pricing

The pricing model is not publicly established. The managed-service positioning suggests retainer-style engagements, but no source confirms this, so it is treated as UNKNOWN.

## 8. Risks

1. **Category crowding**: well-funded competitors with product-led models [S3]
2. **Services margin**: managed delivery scales with headcount
3. **Platform dependence**: AI assistants change ranking and citation behaviour without notice
4. **Evidence gap**: no public customers or revenue signals

## 9. Assessment

Unveilr is a very early bet on a fast-growing category. The managed-service model is a reasonable wedge in a market where buyers lack in-house AEO skills, but the company has not yet published proof points. Confidence in this assessment is **medium-low** because most facts rest on funding coverage.
