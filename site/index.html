Built on evidence

Every claim has a source. Every source is real.

Each brief is researched with live web search. Source links come from the search results themselves — never typed in by a model — and anything that can't be verified is marked as such.

  • Cited inline, with a numbered source list
  • Unknowns are stated, not guessed
  • Delivered as a clean PDF you can forward

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes — Dawnbrief is free during early access.

How many briefs will I get?

One per day, at most. If there's nothing new that fits your profile, you won't get padding — you'll simply hear from us the next day there is.

Why do I need to confirm my email twice?

Signing in confirms your email with us. Because Dawnbrief is in early access, Amazon's email service also sends a one-time confirmation message to your address; click its link once so briefs can reach your inbox. Check spam if you don't see it.

Where does the information come from?

News coverage plus live web research. Each brief lists the sources it used, and states plainly what could not be confirmed.

Can I change my choices or stop?

Anytime. Sign in, edit your role, regions and industries, or pause your briefs with one switch.

Is this investment advice?

No. Dawnbrief is an information service. Briefs summarise public reporting and may contain errors — verify before you act.
